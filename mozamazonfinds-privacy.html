Privacy Policy – Moz Amazon Finds Publisher

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Moz Amazon Finds Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pinterest account @afzrlyman to publish their own Pins and read their own Pin analytics.

Data the app uses

  • The OAuth access token Pinterest issues to the account owner. It is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared.
  • The owner's own Pins, boards and Pin analytics (impressions, saves, outbound clicks), used only to publish Pins and to measure their performance.

Data the app does not collect

  • The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other Pinterest user or of website visitors.
  • The app does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Links

Pins link to product pages on Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon's own privacy policy applies on Amazon's site.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings; the stored token is then deleted.
